- Title: EarthTrend Detective Pro
- Challenge: Be An Earth System Trend Detective!
- Event: NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
- Team: decoder

## Problem

Earth-system variables can trend in different directions in different places. It is difficult to determine what is changing, where it is changing, how much it is changing, and whether the observed evidence is statistically significant.

## Solution

EarthTrend Detective is an evidence-first investigation prototype that uses NASA data to display location-based environmental trends, magnitude, statistical evidence, provenance, and limitations.

## Important limitations

- A gridded returned coordinate is not an exact satellite-pixel observation.
- Spatial output is exploratory context, not independent regional proof.
- Monthly data cannot establish daily event impact.
- Association is not causation.
- Non-significance does not prove that no trend exists.
- Event-source coverage is limited and may be unavailable.
- The complete Builder source tree is not currently included in this repository.
